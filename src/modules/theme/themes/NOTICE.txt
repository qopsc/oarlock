Bundled terminal colour schemes

The terminal palettes of the themes below are taken verbatim from the copies
Ghostty bundles: the ghostty/ directory of iTerm2-Color-Schemes
(https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes, commit 86a776d947b6),
so a scheme looks the same in Oarlock as with `theme = <name>` in Ghostty.
Where a scheme selects by inverting text (Oarlock draws selections as an
overlay), the selection colour comes from the scheme's own editor theme. The
surrounding app colours are taken from each scheme's upstream palette.
